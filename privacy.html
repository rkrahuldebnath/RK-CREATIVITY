Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2025

1. Introduction

Welcome to RK CREATIVITY. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any personal information you may share with us. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your data when you visit our website and use our web applications.

2. Data Collection

We prioritize client-side processing. Most of the tools and games available on RK CREATIVITY (such as the Calculator, Puzzle Games, and Invoice Generator) run entirely within your browser.

  • We do not store your input data (e.g., invoice details, passwords generated) on our servers.
  • Data created within these tools typically remains on your device and is lost when you close the tab, unless explicitly saved by you (e.g., downloading a PDF).

3. Local Storage & Cookies

We use standard web technologies like Local Storage to improve your experience. This is used solely for:

  • Saving your preference for Light or Dark Mode.
  • Saving game high scores or progress locally on your device.

We do not use tracking cookies for advertising purposes.

4. Third-Party Services

Our website may include embedded content from third-party providers, such as:

  • YouTube: For displaying tutorial videos. YouTube may collect data about your interaction with the embedded video player.
  • Google Fonts: For typography styling.

Please review the privacy policies of these third-party services for more information on how they handle your data.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us via the form on our Contact Page or email us at wheelchairrk@gmail.com.
