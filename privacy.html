🔐 Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 1, 2026

At Viveka Digital Hub, we value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights. By using our website, you agree to the terms described below.

01. Information We Collect

When you interact with our website or place an order via WhatsApp, we may collect the following information:

  • Name — provided voluntarily when contacting us or ordering.
  • Phone Number / WhatsApp Number — used to process and deliver your order.
  • Message Content — the text you send via WhatsApp for order processing.
  • Device & Browser Info — basic technical data collected automatically when you visit our site (no personal identification).

We do not collect passwords, payment card details, or any sensitive financial information directly.

02. How We Use Your Information

The information we collect is used solely for the following purposes:

  • To process and deliver your digital product orders via WhatsApp.
  • To respond to your queries and provide customer support.
  • To send you order confirmation and product delivery.
  • To improve our website and product offerings.
  • To inform you about new products, offers, or updates (only if you opt in).

03. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We respect your data and follow strict rules:

  • We do NOT sell, rent, or share your personal data with any third-party companies.
  • We do NOT use your data for advertising or marketing without your consent.
  • Your WhatsApp conversations are protected by WhatsApp's end-to-end encryption.
  • Payment information is handled through secure UPI/payment platforms and is not stored by us.

04. Cookies & Tracking

Our website may use minimal browser storage (localStorage) to remember your applied coupon code during your visit. This data:

  • Is stored only in your browser — never sent to our servers.
  • Is cleared automatically when you close your browser (session-based).
  • Contains no personal identifiable information.

We do not use third-party advertising cookies or tracking pixels.

05. Data Security

We take reasonable precautions to protect your information:

  • All WhatsApp communications are end-to-end encrypted by WhatsApp Inc.
  • We do not store customer data on external servers or cloud databases.
  • Our website does not require you to create an account or share passwords.
  • Order details are only accessed by authorized personnel for fulfillment.

Our services are intended for students of all ages, including school students. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13 without parental consent.
